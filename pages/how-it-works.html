FAQ

Common Questions

What is an eSIM?
An eSIM (embedded SIM) is a digital SIM that allows you to activate a cellular plan without using a physical SIM card. It's built into modern smartphones and can store multiple profiles simultaneously.
Do I need to remove my physical SIM?
No! Your eSIM works alongside your physical SIM. You can keep your regular number for calls and texts while using the Simphonia eSIM for data. Most phones support Dual SIM (one physical + one eSIM).
When should I install the eSIM?
We recommend installing your eSIM before you travel — ideally when you have a WiFi connection. The eSIM will activate automatically when you arrive at your destination and enable data roaming.
Can I top up or extend my plan?
Yes! You can purchase additional data or extend your plan directly from the Simphonia app at any time. Top-ups are applied instantly to your active eSIM.
Is my device compatible with eSIM?
Most modern smartphones support eSIM — including all iPhones from XS onwards, Samsung Galaxy S20+, Google Pixel 3 and later.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an eSIM?
An eSIM (embedded SIM) is a digital SIM built into your smartphone. It allows you to activate a cellular data plan without inserting a physical SIM card. You can store multiple eSIM profiles on your device and switch between them as needed.
Is my phone compatible with eSIM?
Most modern smartphones support eSIM, including:

Apple: iPhone XS, XR and all later models
Samsung: Galaxy S20, Note 20 and later
Google: Pixel 3 and later
Others: Huawei P40+, Motorola Razr, Oppo Find X3+

Check our compatibility checker for a full list.
How do I install my eSIM?
After purchasing a plan, your QR code appears in the Simphonia app. Go to your phone's Settings → Cellular/Mobile Data → Add eSIM, and scan the QR code. Follow the on-screen prompts. The whole process takes about 1 minute. We recommend installing while connected to WiFi, before you travel.
Do I need to remove my physical SIM?
No! eSIM works alongside your physical SIM (Dual SIM). Keep your regular number active for calls and texts, and use Simphonia for mobile data. You can set your eSIM as the default data line in your phone's settings.
When does my eSIM plan start?
Your plan starts when you connect to a network at your destination and begin using data. Simply install the eSIM before you travel, and it will activate automatically when you arrive. This means no wasted days!
Can I top up or extend my plan?
Yes! Open the Simphonia app, go to your active eSIM, and tap "Top Up." Additional data is added instantly to your current plan. You can also purchase a new plan for the same or different destination.
What speeds can I expect?
Simphonia connects you to premium local networks. Most plans offer 4G/LTE speeds, and 5G is available in select destinations. Actual speeds depend on local network conditions, your device, and location.
What is your refund policy?
If you haven't activated your eSIM (i.e., not connected to a network), you can request a full refund within 30 days of purchase. Once data has been used, refunds are evaluated on a case-by-case basis. Contact our support team for assistance.
Can I use hotspot/tethering?
Most of our plans support personal hotspot/tethering, allowing you to share your data connection with other devices. Check the plan details before purchase — it's clearly marked whether hotspot is included.
Can I make calls and send texts?
Our eSIM plans are data-only. You can make calls and send messages using apps like WhatsApp, FaceTime, Telegram, or Skype over your data connection. Your physical SIM keeps your regular number active for traditional calls.
